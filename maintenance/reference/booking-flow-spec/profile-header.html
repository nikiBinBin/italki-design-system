About me

¡Hola! I'm Lucía, a DELE examiner with 8 years of classroom experience in Madrid and 6 years teaching online. Every lesson is built around what you need to say next week — a job interview, a trip, a conversation with your in-laws — not around a textbook chapter.

I hold a degree in Hispanic Philology from Universidad Complutense and a DELE examiner accreditation (A1–C1). My students range from complete beginners to diplomats preparing for postings in Latin America. Outside class you'll find me cooking my grandmother's recipes, hiking the Sierra de Guadarrama, or arguing about football. Classes are relaxed but never improvised — you'll always leave with notes, corrections and homework you can actually finish.
